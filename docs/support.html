Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Pocket Anchor has no sign-in, no accounts, and nothing to set up.

Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Everything stays on your device — see the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens when I activate a session?
Your screen turns dark and quiet. If you picked a timed option, you'll hear one soft chime at the halfway point — nothing else is required. Tap "I'm back" whenever you return.

Can I skip the noticing prompts?
Yes — everything past choosing a duration is optional. Nothing is required, tracked, or scored.

I'm not receiving the halfway chime.
Check that Pocket Anchor has notification permission in Settings → Notifications → Pocket Anchor, and that your device isn't in Do Not Disturb / Focus mode that blocks it.

Pocket Anchor is part of the Life Forecast family of calm, private, local-only apps, alongside EchoSink and Downstep.

Privacy Policy

Pocket Anchor · Last updated September 22, 2026

The short version: Pocket Anchor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, anywhere, ever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no network connection of any kind. Everything the app remembers lives only on your device.

What Pocket Anchor stores

Pocket Anchor keeps a small, local record of your outings (the duration you chose and when you went) so the app can show you a summary when you return. This record is stored using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework, entirely within your phone's local storage.

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server we operate, not sent to us, and not accessible to us in any form — we simply don't have it. If your device has iCloud backup enabled, this data may be included in your personal iCloud backup, which is between you and Apple; we have no access to it there either.

What Pocket Anchor does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests — the app functions entirely offline
  • No location, microphone, camera, or contacts access

Notifications

If you allow it, Pocket Anchor schedules a single local notification (a quiet chime) at the halfway point of a timed outing. This is handled entirely by iOS on your device and involves no server or external service.

Children's privacy

Pocket Anchor does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.
